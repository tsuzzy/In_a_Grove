Treating an event history as an optimization variable — and letting a simulator, not a story, decide which accounts the evidence can rule out.

A samurai lies dead of a single dagger wound. A bandit, the wife, and the dead man each tell a different tale. Which tales can the evidence actually refute — and what does self-interest do with the room that is left?

Assign each one an objective from the menu; each settles on the account that flatters them most. What makes this real

The variable is the history, not the prose

Each account is a structured history — who takes the dagger, who cuts the rope, who strikes whom — and whether it is possible is settled by replaying it through a simulator, never by how well it is told. And the winners differ from Akutagawa's canon (the wife's best account is that her husband fell upon the blade, not her confession) — so the divergence is an emergent optimum, not a memorized tale.

Method

Propose, verify, optimize

Propose

Candidate histories

By exhaustive enumeration on a small domain; by an LLM proposing action sequences on a large one. The multi-agent generative prior lives here.

Verify

The simulator, not a model

Replay each candidate through the domain and diff against F. A memorized or hallucinated history that breaks the physics is rejected here, whatever produced it.

Optimize

argmax over the feasible set

A transparent structural Ui — decisive or passive? dignified or helpless? clean hands? — scores each history. It names no manner of death; the manner, if it wins, is emergent.

Honest notes

The objective is hand-specified. The weights are fixed a priori and shown in the objective menus above; a learned or human-elicited Ui would be stronger. But the feasibility verdicts — the part that refutes the duel — come entirely from the simulator and depend on none of this.

Robust, not universal. Across 5,000 random weightings of the same generic dimensions the full three-way spread holds in 74.3% of cases and the bandit's ideal stays refuted in 69.0%. Modal, not an artifact of one weighting — press the button above and it recomputes with the paper's seed.

Robust to the constants, not just the weights. Perturbing all nineteen hand-set feature values independently by ±ε (uniform, N=5,000 per level, seed 16980222) leaves the three-way spread and the bandit's exclusion intact well past small perturbations — the split is a property of the feasible-set geometry, not of the particular constants:

Per-model detail (prompted baselines). Averaged across the three witnesses the three models agree within 6 points, yet fall up to 32 short of the simulator; on a single witness the between-model spread reaches 38. 125 of 135 generations yield an extractable signature, all in the released data.

One scene, so far. Results are on a single literary domain, small enough to enumerate exactly. The framework applies wherever a simulator can adjudicate multi-agent histories — negotiation, social-deduction games, incident reconstruction — where incentive-driven misreport is native rather than staged. The source concerns sexual violence; it is treated here only as contested testimony.

Reproducibility. Every number in the paper except the prompted baseline rows is produced by deterministic, language-model-free code: optima are exact (Feas(F) enumerated exhaustively), Monte-Carlo figures use the fixed seed 16980222 (mulberry32), and bracketed intervals are Wilson 95% at N=5,000. The released code regenerates every number and figure — figures recompute from the same modules that produce the tables, so they cannot go stale — and also carries the full scoring rules for all three domains, the coupled objective Uitot with its results, the per-model prompting baselines, the order-dependence analysis, and three further objectives (spectator, adjudicating, other-regarding) over the identical Feas(F); self-tests total 420 assertions. Code, figures, studies, scripts: github.com/tsuzzy/2026-grove-again.

Each witness averages a fixed triple, then ×100. Bandit uses honour, agency, clean hands. Wife uses sympathy, dignity, clean hands. Samurai uses honour, agency, wronged. So Ui is (100/3) times the sum of its three values. The words “duel”, “suicide”, “honour-killing” appear in no rule. A manner wins only as an emergent consequence of these generic scores.
