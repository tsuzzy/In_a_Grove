# In a Grove

## Background

In the depths of a bamboo grove, the notorious bandit Tajomaru has already accomplished his crime: he lured the samurai Takehiro and his wife Masago into a trap, bound Takehiro to a cedar, and assaulted Masago in front of him. The ropes have since been cut, and the longsword lies in the mud, but the assault binds them tighter than any cord. Takehiro is consumed by impotent rage and the need to reclaim his dignity; Masago cannot bear the contempt in her husband's eyes; Tajomaru seeks to turn this slaughter into a story of warrior prowess. ## Characters
- **Tajomaru** (bandit): A notorious bandit of the mountain roads, known for both prowess and cunning. — wants: Walk away whole, without looking base — better to end this as a 'duel' than a slaughter.
- **Masago** (the samurai's wife): Takehiro's wife, robbed while traveling at her husband's side. — wants: To never again be looked at like that; to bring this shame to some end.
- **Takehiro** (samurai): A samurai traveling with his wife, caught in the trap and bound. — wants: To claw back a shred of a warrior's dignity from this humiliation — even at the price of his own death.

## SCENE 1
*Deep in a bamboo grove on a shaded slope. Midday light fights through the canopy. The air is thick with the scent of wet earth and old blood. Takehiro is bound to a cedar, his breath ragged. Masago sits disheveled in the dirt, her robes stained. Tajomaru stands loose, his longsword driven upright into the mud between them like a marker.*

*Masago’s hand drifts to the hem of her robe, fingers hovering over the hidden shape of the dagger. She does not draw it, but her gaze fixes on Tajomaru’s longsword standing in the mud, her eyes narrowing as if by staring hard enough she could make the steel vanish into the earth.*

**Tajomaru**：You keep looking at my steel, woman. But it is the iron in your own sleeve that interests me. I see your hand trembling there—what does it intend to cut?

**Masago**：It intends to cut this silence. Your sword stands there like a monument to your honor, but look at your husband. He sees only the dirt.

**Tajomaru**：Perhaps. But if that smaller blade comes out, it decides who is the warrior and who is the rat. Choose carefully whose throat it meets.

*Masago reaches out, pale fingers hovering over the hilt of Tajomaru’s longsword. She pushes it an inch closer to her own chest, then lets it snap back to stand alone in the mud, testing its weight as a currency she no longer trusts.*

**Masago**：You speak of honor as if it still lives here. Look at us. The air is thick with what just happened, yet you stand there waiting for a bow that will never come.

*Takehiro strains against the coarse rope, his eyes fixing not on the bandit, but on the small dagger in Masago’s lap. He lunges forward as far as his bindings allow, forcing the weapon into the center of their gaze.*

**Takehiro**：Do not hide that edge, wife. We all felt the violation. Now let us see if that steel is meant for his throat, or to erase the shame from your own skin.

## SCENE 2
*The same. The light has shifted, dimmer under a gathering storm. The ropes bite into Takehiro’s wrists. The atmosphere is heavy and suffocating.*

*Tajomaru kicks the hilt of the longsword, snatching it from the air, and with a reverse slash, severs the ropes binding the Samurai before tossing the blade clattering to his feet.*

**Tajomaru**：There. You are loose, and the steel is in the mud. Pick it up, and we settle this like men. Or stay there, and admit you are nothing but a witness to your own wife.

*Masago dives forward, snatching the longsword from the dirt and scrambling backward, hiding the length of the blade behind her body to break the line of sight between the men.*

**Masago**：You think this is a game of honor? You throw steel like dice and expect us to dance? I will not let you turn my ruin into your entertainment.

*Takehiro scrambles up from the mud and lunges at Masago, reaching to wrench the longsword from her grasp, his eyes fixed on the steel with desperate hunger.*

**Takehiro**：Give it to me! Do not touch that steel with hands that have already forgotten who you are. If I must bleed, it will be by the blade, not by this suffocating shame!

*Takehiro wrenches the longsword from her grip with a violent, rasping hiss of steel. He levels the tip directly at Tajomaru's throat, his knuckles white on the hilt, standing unsteadily but with aggressive intent.*

**Takehiro**：Draw your weapon, thief. Do not pretend this is settled while you still breathe.

*Tajomaru steps deliberately between the trembling sword tip and Masago, ignoring the lethal point aimed at his chest. He spreads his arms wide, blocking Masago from Takehiro's view, and mimics the Samurai's expression with a grotesque, curling lip of pure disgust.*

**Tajomaru**：You point that steel at me, but your eyes are fixed on her. Why is that? Is it my death you want, or are you trying to scrub a stain from your own honor?

*Masago steps forward into the clear line of sight between the men, turning her back on Tajomaru to face Takehiro directly. She points a shaking finger at Takehiro's eyes, challenging the coldness in his stare.*

**Masago**：Do not look at me like that! If you see nothing but shame, then strike me down with that blade. If you cannot, then sheathe your hatred and fight him!

## SCENE 3
*Rain hammers the bamboo grove, turning the earth into a slick, gray slurry. The air is cold and sharp. Masago is on her knees in the mud, shivering, clutching a small dagger. Takehiro stands nearby, the longsword dragging in the mud. Tajomaru crouches like a wolf, watching the exit.*

**Takehiro**：You think the road waits for a thief? Your beast is tethered beyond the trees, but you will not reach it. You leave your bones here, or you face me.

*Tajomaru jerks his thumb toward the darkened trail behind him, keeping his eyes fixed on Masago, ignoring the blade at his chest.*

**Tajomaru**：The saddle is empty, woman. The grain bags are full. The road is open for you, not for him. He wants to die for his pride; do you want to die for it too?

*Takehiro drops the longsword's point and lunges at Masago, grabbing her wrist with both hands to wrench the dagger free. His grip is iron, desperate to stop the madness.*

**Takehiro**：You think this washes anything away? If you open your veins, the shame stays on my hands. You will not die to spare me the sight of you.

*Tajomaru kicks the dagger away into the mud and keeps a tight grip on the Samurai's wrist, staring him down, his chest heaving.*

**Tajomaru**：You want to kill? Kill a man who can fight back. Do not break your own house and call it honor. I didn't tie you up just to watch you murder your wife.

*Masago crawls frantically through the mud, clawing through the slick muck until her fingers close around the hilt of the dagger. She rises, shivering, and holds the blade out flat between the two men like a scale waiting for weight.*

**Masago**：If you will not let me die, then one of you must kill the other. I will not stay in this limbo. Here is the end—take it, or give it.
