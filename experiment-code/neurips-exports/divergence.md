# 同一开局,2 条命运

> 开局对所有 2 次完全相同(见下),没有人为每条结局写过一个字。差异全部来自 agent 各自的隐藏目标与逐回合博弈。

**固定开局**:The bandit Tajomaru lured the samurai Takehiro and his wife Masago into the grove with the bait of an old sword, bound Takehiro to a cedar, and assaulted Masago before his eyes. It is done. The three are frozen in place: the longsword stands in the mud, Masago has a small dagger, the samurai is tied. No one has moved yet. What happens next is unwritten.

核心冲突:Who walks out of this grove alive, and into whose body the blade finally goes

---

## 第 1 次 — 结局

*The longsword lies hidden in the shadows, removing the possibility of a clean warrior's death. Masago refuses to let the bandit dictate the terms of an honorable exit, while Takehiro, bound and stripped of dignity, forces the reality of the concealed dagger into the light. The three remain locked in the grove: the horse waits outside, the sword is lost to the dark, and the small dagger presses against the wife's ribs*

## 第 2 次 — 结局

*The grove is silent, save for the heavy breathing of the men. Masago has vanished into the bamboo with the longsword, refusing to let the men dictate her death or her shame. Takehiro remains bound to the cedar, the dagger concealed in his lap, his eyes fixed on the darkness with a look of mingled hatred and possessiveness. Tajomaru stands alone in the clearing, his weapon gone, facing a samurai who may yet die by his*
