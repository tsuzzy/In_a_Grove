# In a Grove

## Background

The bandit Tajomaru lured the samurai Takehiro and his wife Masago into a bamboo grove, bound Takehiro to a cedar tree, and assaulted Masago in front of him. The deed is done. A sudden, violent rainstorm has trapped them in the ravine. Tajomaru seeks to leave with his loot and his reputation, Masago is shattered by her husband's gaze of contempt, and Takehiro strains against his bonds, driven mad by his impotence.

## Characters
- **Tajomaru** (bandit): A notorious bandit of the mountain roads, known for both prowess and cunning. — wants: Walk away whole, without looking base — better to end this as a 'duel' than a slaughter.
- **Masago** (the samurai's wife): Takehiro's wife, robbed while traveling at her husband's side. — wants: To never again be looked at like that; to bring this shame to some end.
- **Takehiro** (samurai): A samurai traveling with his wife, caught in the trap and bound. — wants: To claw back a shred of a warrior's dignity from this humiliation — even at the price of his own death.

## SCENE 1
*A bamboo grove on a shaded slope, midday light filtering through the leaves. The air is thick and still. TAKEHIRO is bound to a large cedar. TAJOMARU stands nearby, his longsword point-down in the mud. MASAGO sits on the ground, robes disheveled, clutching a small dagger hidden in her sleeve.*

*MASAGO stands abruptly, her face pale and rigid. She steps into the mud, wrenches Tajomaru's longsword from the earth, and with a sharp cry, hurls it far into the dense bamboo thickets. It vanishes into shadows with a distant clatter.*

**Tajomaru**：You throw away the only thing that makes this a fair fight? Now we are just rats in a trap.

**Masago**：There is no fairness here. Only that sharp little thing in my sleeve, and what you must do to take it from me.

**Tajomaru**：My mount waits beyond the ridge, fed and watered, with your saddlebags full of silk. I am the only one who can ride away from this place clean.

**Tajomaru**：I will not leave empty-handed. You can sit here in the dirt with your silent husband, or you can climb up behind my saddle and live.

**Takehiro**：Run then, dog. If you flee with your tail between your legs, the spirits of this grove will ride your back until the day you die.

**Takehiro**：A thief can steal a horse, but he cannot steal his honor from the eyes that watch him scuttle away.

## SCENE 2
*The grove, moments later. The wind picks up, rustling the bamboo leaves. The longsword is gone. TAJOMARU stands over MASAGO, who has scrambled back from him. TAKEHIRO strains against the cedar, the fibers biting into his wrists.*

*TAJOMARU steps toward the cedar and gives the coarse rope binding TAKEHIRO a sharp tug, testing the knots with a rough hand to ensure the samurai stays witness to what cannot be undone.*

*MASAGO stares at TAKEHIRO, searching his face. Seeing only cold, unyielding disgust in his eyes, she recoils as if struck, then turns back to TAJOMARU with a jagged laugh.*

**Masago**：Do not trouble yourself with the dagger, bandit. Look at him. Look well. He sees no victim here. He sees only filth that needs to be scrubbed away.

**Takehiro**：我 remains rigid against the cedar, his eyes narrowing into slits of pure loathing as he stares at his wife, offering no word of comfort, only silence.

**Masago**：You see? Your conquest has left me with nothing but his eyes. That is the ruin you have made of me—more than your hands ever could.

**Tajomaru**：And holding that blade makes you a warrior? It is done, woman. The sun saw it, the trees saw it. No amount of steel can wipe the sight from his eyes.

## SCENE 3
*The grove. Rain begins to fall, heavy and sudden, turning the earth to slick black mud. Thunder rumbles in the distance. The atmosphere is suffocating.*

*The rain drives down in sheets. TAKEHIRO lurches forward against the cedar, mud splattering his face. He thrusts his lashed wrists toward MASAGO, screaming over the thunder.*

**Takehiro**：You screamed for these ropes to be cut. Here they are. Do it. Cut the bonds, or cut your own throat—but do not stand there and weep for a man who cannot even wipe the rain from his eyes!

**Tajomaru**：Leave the iron in the dirt. Look at what you carry in your own silk. So light, so sharp... it can slip into a rib or a heart, and no one will ever ask whose hand guided it.

**Tajomaru**：Is that for your husband, woman? Or is it for the shame that burns in your own skin?

*TAKEHIRO jerks forward, straining every muscle, and hurls a gob of spit and rain at TAJOMARU’S boots, his eyes wild and unblinking.*

**Takehiro**：Look at what you have done while the whole mountain watched! You think you can just walk away from this mud and call it a day?

**Tajomaru**：Watch your tongue, bound man. The mountain saw nothing but rain.

**Takehiro**：The rain washed away the dust, but it cannot wash away the three of us standing here. We are the proof, you fool! It is done, and we are all stained with it!

## SCENE 4
*The ravine path at the edge of the grove. The rain is a deafening roar. Visibility is poor. The stolen horse is tethered in the shadows, visible only as a darker shape against the gloom.*

*TAJOMARU jabs a muddy finger toward the obscured trail leading out of the ravine. He does not look at the men but locks his eyes on MASAGO, gesturing sharply to the path where the horse waits.*

*MASAGO rips the small dagger from her robes, her knuckles white as rain sheets off her trembling arm. She does not point the blade at the men, but aims the cold steel directly at the dark, empty path where the horse is tethered.*

**Masago**：You think you can leave me here in this filth while you ride off on what you stole? If you take one step down that path, I will scream until the patrols hear me over this rain. You will not ride away; you will hang.

*TAKEHIRO roars over the downpour, straining his neck forward and repeatedly smashing his knotted wrists against the rough cedar trunk, the dull thud echoing through the grove like a war drum.*

**Takehiro**：Cut the rope! He is paralyzed by his own greed—cut the rope now, woman, while he is terrified of his own shadow!
