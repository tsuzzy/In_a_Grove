Agent Society · The Optimised Witness

Now the argmax is actually computed

The witness layer measured self-interest; it did not optimise for it. Here it does. For each witness we draw K accounts from incentive alone, discard every one a coroner could refute (enforcing the hard constraint τ∈F), and keep the feasible account that best serves the teller. The witnesses may now lie only where the evidence is silent — and yet they still reach three irreconcilable pasts.

Solved for real: τ̂i = argmaxτ Ui(τ)  s.t.  τ ∈ F, by best-of-K rejection sampling. Two quantities fall out: Δopt = U(winner) − mean U — the self-interest we injected by selecting, the authorship the optimiser adds; and lift-from-lying — how much a discarded provable lie would have bought, the temptation the constraint refuses.

● feasible draw (∈F) ○ discarded — a provable lie (∉F) ◉ the winner (hard-constrained argmax)
