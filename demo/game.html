When the witnesses can hear each other coming

Every earlier layer solved each witness alone: each picked the account that best served them, and only afterwards did we notice the three were irreconcilable. Nobody ever anticipated anybody. Here they do. The bandit guesses the wife will blame the husband — and rewrites his own story around that guess. Testimony stops being three optimisations and becomes one game.

Independent: τ̂i = argmaxτ∈F Ui(τ)  →  coupled: τ̂i = BRi(τ̂−i), solved by level-k bounded rationality over the same simulator-decided feasible set. Each witness now also wants to be corroborated: Uitot = (1−β)·Ui + β·credi. At β=0 this collapses exactly to the previous layer, so the old result is not replaced — it is the k=0 row.
