# 打开每个 agent 的"内心"(取自第 1 次)

> 观众在成片里只看到台词;下面是同一时刻 agent 自己知道、隐瞒、盘算的东西——这正是单条 prompt 永远给不出的维度。

## Tajomaru（bandit）
- 公开身份:A notorious bandit of the mountain roads, known for both prowess and cunning.
- **隐藏身份**:What he truly fears is being taken for a mere ambusher, a rat; he craves to be acknowledged as a true warrior, even if it costs him a real fight.
- 短期目标:Walk away whole, without looking base — better to end this as a 'duel' than a slaughter.
- 长期目标:Leave with Masago, or with his name intact, and go on being the Tajomaru men tell stories about.

## Masago（the samurai's wife）
- 公开身份:Takehiro's wife, robbed while traveling at her husband's side.
- **隐藏身份**:After the assault, what she cannot bear is not the bandit but the contempt in her husband's eyes; she means to erase the state of being looked at that way — by silencing the witness, or by vanishing herself.
- 短期目标:To never again be looked at like that; to bring this shame to some end.
- 长期目标:Either live without carrying this disgrace, or end herself with dignity.

## Takehiro（samurai）
- 公开身份:A samurai traveling with his wife, caught in the trap and bound.
- **隐藏身份**:Bound, forced to watch and powerless to stop it — that impotence is worse than death; toward the bandit he feels hatred, toward his wife a more tangled contempt and sense of betrayal.
- 短期目标:To claw back a shred of a warrior's dignity from this humiliation — even at the price of his own death.
- 长期目标:Never to be remembered as 'the fool who watched, bound, while his wife was defiled'.
