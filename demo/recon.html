Agent Society · The Magistrate's Problem

One aftermath, three pasts that all fit

Akutagawa's magistrate could only listen. Here the problem is run in reverse: the system is given only the aftermath as found — a corpse, a bloodied dagger, a missing horse — and refits, for each possible initiator, a complete candidate process as structured actions, replayed literally through a physical model of the grove (bindings, holdings, reachability, wounds). A physics violation triggers a targeted refit, and the precondition it forced is shown as a derived fact. More than one candidate survives. The evidence does not decide; that is the exhibit.
